\section{Reproducibility}
Code, configuration, pinned requirements, per-query results and generated tables are in the accompanying repository, archived as release v0.1.0 at
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23351799}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.23351799} (\texttt{make quick} reruns everything; \texttt{make report} regenerates every table, figure and number in this paper
from the stored results). Landmark extraction versions and timings are logged, and every result file has a provenance file with
the commit and library versions. The pre-registration is \texttt{PREREG.md}; deviations from it are listed there.
Derived hand and pose landmarks from PopSign are not part of the v0.1.0 archive; if released later it will be under CC~BY~4.0 with attribution to Georgia Tech and the Deaf Professional Arts
Network. Videos and face landmarks are not released. ASL Citizen landmarks and per-query results are not released (licence); its aggregate tables, figures and macros are
generated by the same \texttt{make report} command from committed aggregates.

\paragraph{Use of AI tools.} An AI coding assistant (Claude, Anthropic) helped write code, tests and a first draft of the text
and checked references. The author specified the design, reviewed and ran the code, checked every number against the generated
results files and every citation against its publisher or arXiv record, and takes responsibility for the content.
