\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One dataset, one language.} All results are for ASL on PopSign (47 signers, selfie phone video, mostly one-handed
signs). We do not test other datasets, languages, cameras or continuous signing.
\item \textbf{Few signers.} The test split has 8 signers, so signer-clustered intervals are wide and small differences are not
resolvable. Training noise (3 seeds) is smaller than signer variation.
\item \textbf{Small models, limited tuning.} The learned models are small and tuned on a small grid at one condition
(vocabulary 50, 10 examples per sign). All three chose the longest epoch budget in the grid, so longer training might help
them further, and the step cap of 4000 gives the largest conditions only a few epochs. Larger models or pre-training may change
the picture. We include one augmentation arm to test sensitivity at one vocabulary only.
\item \textbf{Only F1 in the main grid, and a handicap risk for DTW.} The main comparison uses the hand-angle features of the
earlier implementation, which carry no hand location or orientation. DTW and Euclidean matching each chose among the preprocessing
options listed in Section~\ref{sec:methods} on validation signers, but other options, local costs or learned metrics might favour template
matching more.
\item \textbf{Landmark quality.} Results depend on MediaPipe 0.10.14 and on the resize used; the fraction of videos or frames
without detected hands is reported in the tables. Face and other non-manual signals are mostly not modelled.
\item \textbf{Not translation, not an application.} Top-1 accuracy on isolated signs is not communication access. We did not
evaluate any system with Deaf users.
\item \textbf{No phonological analysis.} PopSign has no phonological annotation; we report data-driven confusions and make no
claim about phonological similarity.
\item \textbf{Timing.} Latency was measured on one Apple M4 Max laptop with other processes running; no low-power device was
available, so embedded performance is not measured.
\item \textbf{Pre-registration scope.} The plan was written by the author before test scoring, but is not a registered report
and was not independently time-stamped; the git history is the record.
\end{itemize}
